\begin{defi}
(Spin-orbit interaction) We call the splitting of energy levels due to the spin-orbit interactions the fine structure. The magnetic dipole energy due to the magnetic field is
\[-\vec{\mu }_{e}\cdot \vec{B}'=-\Big(\dfrac{-e g}{2m_{e}}\Big)\vec{S}\cdot \vec{B}'\]
by subtituting the magnetic potential, we find
\[\begin{align*}
-\vec{\mu }_{e}\cdot \vec{B}'=&\dfrac{eg}{2m_{e}}\vec{S}\cdot \Big(\dfrac{\vec{v}}{c^2}\times \dfrac{\vec{r}}{r}\dfrac{d \phi }{d r} \Big)\\
=&-\dfrac{eg}{2m_{e}^2c^2}\vec{S}\cdot \vec{L}\Big(\dfrac{1}{r}\dfrac{d \phi }{d r} \Big)
\end{align*}\]
where $\vec{L}=\vec{r}\times (m_{e}\vec{v})$. When done properly, we find Thomas precession, giving
\[\phi (\vec{r})=\Big(\dfrac{ze}{r}\dfrac{1}{4\pi \varepsilon _{0}}\Big)\]
This ultimately gives
\[H_{SO}=\dfrac{1}{2}\Big(\dfrac{g}{2}\Big)\dfrac{ze^2}{4\pi \varepsilon _{0}}\dfrac{1}{2m_{e}^2c^2}\dfrac{\vec{S}\cdot \vec{L}}{r^3}\]
In the Hydrogen atom, now consider the eigenvectors $|n,l\rangle $, in addition to the spin as $|n,l,m,6\rangle $. We find $2(2l+1)$ degenerate levels. Recall that we simply need to diagonalise the matrix for degenerate levels
\[\langle l,m',6'|H_{SO}|l,m,6\rangle \]
Also recall that
\[H_{SO}\propto \vec{S}\cdot \vec{L}=\dfrac{1}{2}\Big(\vec{J}^2-\vec{L}^2-\vec{S}^2\Big)\]
and therefore 
\[|l,m,5\rangle \rightarrow |l,j,m_{j}\rangle \]
should be the basis we should consider and it would be automatically diagonalised. Recall the following formula
\[|j=l+\dfrac{1}{2},m+\dfrac{1}{2}\rangle =\sqrt{\dfrac{l+m+1}{2l+1}}|l,m\,\uparrow\rangle +\sqrt{\dfrac{l-m}{2l+1}}|l,m+1\,\downarrow\rangle \]

\end{defi}

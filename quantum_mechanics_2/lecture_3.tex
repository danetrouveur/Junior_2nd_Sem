\section{Lecture 3 (September 8th)}
\begin{defi}
(Pertubation theory) In pertubation theory, an arbitrary Hamiltonian is obtained through writing
\[H(t)=H_{0}+\lambda H_{1}(t)\]
where we fully know ${H}_{0}$ analytically. In general, we deal with
\begin{itemize}
\item[(i)] Time independent pertubations
\item[(ii)] Time dependent pertubations
\end{itemize}
\end{defi}
\vspace{2ex}
\begin{ex}
(Non-degenerate pertubation) Given the information of ${H}_{0}$ we want to inquire the following,
	\[E_{n}^{(0)}\quad\&\quad |n^{(0)}\rangle\qquad\implies \qquad |n\rangle _{\lambda }\quad\&\quad E_{n} \]
 where $\hat{H}|n\rangle _{\lambda }=E_{n}|n\rangle _{\lambda }$. We are to of course expect the following relationship for an arbitrary function $\mathrm{\Delta }_{n}(\lambda )$
\[E_{n}= E_{n}^{(0)}+\mathrm{\Delta }_{n}(\lambda )\]
and thus
\[(\hat{H}_{0}-E_{n}^{(0)})|n\rangle _{\lambda }=(\mathrm{\Delta }_{n}(\lambda )-\lambda \hat{V})|n\rangle _{\lambda }\]
As $H_{0}|n^{(0)}\rangle =E_{n}^{(0)}|n^{(0)}\rangle $, $\langle n^{(0)}|(H_{0}-E_{n}^{(0)})=0$ which leads to
\[\langle n^{(0)}|\mathrm{\Delta }_{n}(\lambda )-\lambda \hat{V}|n\rangle _{\lambda }=0\]
Telling us that $|n^{(0)}\rangle $ is missing from the ket of the right-hand side. Now define the projection from
\[{\bf 1}=\sum _{k}|k^{(0)}\rangle \langle k^{(0)}|\]
where the Hilbert space $\mathcal{H}$ has the basis $\{|k^{(0)}\rangle  \}$ as
\[\phi _{n}={\bf 1}-|n^{(0)}\rangle \langle n^{(0)}|=\sum _{k\ne n}|k^{(0)}\rangle \langle k^{(0)}|\]
givens some $n$. Now,
	\[\boxed{|n\rangle _{\lambda }=C_{n}(\lambda )|n^{(0)}\rangle +\dfrac{1}{E_{n}^{(0)}-\hat{H}_{0}}\phi _{n}(-\mathrm{\Delta  }_{n}(\lambda )+\lambda \hat{V})|n\rangle _{\lambda }}\]
Notice that $\langle n^{(0)}|n\rangle _{\lambda }=C_{n}(\lambda )$ and that $C_{n}(\lambda )$ dictates normalisation (how much $|n^{(0)}\rangle $ is in $|n\rangle _{\lambda }$). We expect the following
	\[|n\rangle _{\lambda }=C_{n}(\lambda )\Big(|n^{(0)}\rangle +\lambda |n^{(1)}\rangle +\lambda ^2|n^{(2)}\rangle +\ldots \Big)\]
now we of course want to substitute. 
\end{ex}

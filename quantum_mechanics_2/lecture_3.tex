\section{Lecture 3 (September 8th)}
\begin{defi}
(Pertubation theory) In pertubation theory, an arbitrary Hamiltonian is obtained through writing
\[H(t)=H_{0}+\lambda H_{1}(t)\]
where we fully know ${H}_{0}$ analytically. In general, we deal with
\begin{itemize}
\item[(i)] Time independent pertubations
\item[(ii)] Time dependent pertubations
\end{itemize}
\end{defi}
\vspace{2ex}
\begin{ex}
(Non-degenerate pertubation) Given the information of ${H}_{0}$ we want to inquire the following,
	\[E_{n}^{(0)}\quad\&\quad |\phi _{n}\rangle \qquad\implies \qquad E_{n}(\lambda )\quad\&\quad |\psi _{n}\rangle  \]
 where $H(\lambda )|\phi _{n}\rangle =E_{n}(\lambda )|\phi _{n}\rangle $ (we omit the hats on operators from now on). We are now going to preform a power series on the energy levels, expected to follow the following relationship for the series $\mathrm{\Delta }_{n}(\lambda )$:
\[E_{n}(\lambda )= E_{n}^{(0)}+\underbrace{\lambda E_{n}^{(1)}+\lambda ^2E_{n}^{(2)}+\ldots}_{\mathrm{\Delta }_{n}(\lambda )} \]
By noticing that $E_{n}(\lambda )|\psi  _{n}\rangle =(H_0+\lambda H_{1})|\psi _{n}\rangle $ and equating it with the above expression, we find 
\[({H}_{0}-E_{n}^{(0)})|\psi _{n}\rangle =(\mathrm{\Delta }_{n}(\lambda )-\lambda H_{1})|\psi _{n}\rangle \]
Also, as $H_{0}|\phi _{n}\rangle  =E_{n}^{(0)}|\phi _{n}\rangle $, we can find its dual $\langle \phi _{n}|(H_{0}-E_{n}^{(0)})=0$ which leads to the conclusion
\[\langle \phi _{n}|\mathrm{\Delta }_{n}(\lambda )-\lambda H_1|\psi _{n}\rangle =0\]
What does this tell us? It tells us that $|\phi _{n}\rangle $ is missing from the ket of the right-hand side. Now define the projection onto $|\phi _{n}\rangle ^{\perp}$ through
\[{\bf 1}=\sum _{k}|\phi _{k}\rangle \langle \phi _{k}|\]
where the Hilbert space $\mathcal{H}$ has the basis $\{|\phi _{k}\rangle  \}$ as
\[\mathrm{Proj}_{n}={\bf 1}-|\phi _{n}\rangle \langle \phi _{n}|=\sum _{k\ne n}|\phi _{k}\rangle \langle \phi _{k}|\]
givens some $n$. Now, we could operate this on the equation above to obtain
	\[\boxed{|\psi _{n}\rangle =N_{n}(\lambda )|\phi _{n}\rangle +(E_{n}^{(0)}-H_0)^{-1}\mathrm{Proj}_{n}(-\mathrm{\Delta  }_{n}(\lambda )+\lambda H_1)|\psi _{n}\rangle }\]
Notice that $\langle \phi _{n}|\psi _{n}\rangle =C_{n}(\lambda )$ and that $C_{n}(\lambda )$ dictates normalisation (how much $|\phi _{n}\rangle $ is in $|\psi _{n}\rangle $). Meanwhile, we expect the following	\[|\psi _{n}\rangle =N_{n}(\lambda )\Big(\underbrace{|\phi _{n}\rangle}_{|\phi _{n}^{()}} +\lambda \underbrace{\sum _{k\ne n}C_{1k} |\phi _{k}\rangle}_{|\phi _{n}^{(1)}\rangle } +\lambda ^2\underbrace{\sum _{k\ne n}C_{2k}|\phi _{k}\rangle }_{|\phi _{n}^{(2)}\rangle }+\ldots \Big)\]
now we of course want to substitute and find each ket in the expansion!
\end{ex}

\section{Lecture 24 (December 3rd)}
\begin{cor}
We would like to observe the different channels in Bhabha scattering in the massless limit. Recall that $\bar{\psi }_{L,R}=\bar{\psi }P_{R,L}$. The decomposition of the QED interaction vertex is:
\[\begin{align*}
eA_{\mu }\bar{\psi }\gamma ^{\mu }\psi =eA_{\mu }\bar{\psi }\gamma ^{\mu }(P_{L}+P_{R})\psi =&eA_{\mu }\bar{\psi }\gamma ^{\mu }(P_{L}P_{L}\psi +P_{R}P_{R})\psi\\ =&eA_{\mu }\bar{\psi }_{L}\gamma ^{\mu }\psi _{L}+eA_{\mu }\bar{\psi }_{R}\gamma ^{\mu }\psi _{R}
\end{align*}\]
The decomposition of $\mathcal{M}_{e^{-}e^{+}\rightarrow e^{-}e^{+}}$ is 
\[\begin{align*}
M_{e^{-}e^{+}\rightarrow e^{-}e^{+}}=&\dfrac{e^2(\bar{U}_{-}\gamma ^{\mu }U_{+})(\bar{U}_{+}\gamma _{\mu }U_{-})}{s^2}-\dfrac{e^2(\bar{U}_{+}\gamma ^{\mu }U_{+})(\bar{U}_{-}\gamma _{\mu }U_{-})}{t^2}
\end{align*}\]
Let's make some comments:
\begin{itemize}
\item[(i)] If you sum all $s$-channels, we yield 
\[2e^{4}\dfrac{t^2+u^2}{^2}\]
as expected.
\item[(ii)] No cross terms between different helicity structures. This can be easily checked through chirality decomposed spin-state sum.
\item[(iii)] Parity invarience of massless QED, and $|\mathcal{M}|^2$ is invariant under $L\leftrightarrow R$. 
\item[(iv)] In the CM frame,
\[\begin{cases}
e_{L}^{-}e_{R}^{+}\rightarrow e_{L}^{-}e_{R}^{+}\big|_{s}\sim \dfrac{u^2}{s^2}\sim \cos ^{4}(\theta /2)\\\\
e_{L}^{-}e_{R}^{+}\rightarrow e^{-}_{R}e_{L}^{-}\big|_{s}\sim \dfrac{t^2}{s^2}\sim \sin ^{4}(\theta /2)
\end{cases}\]
which goes to zero as $\theta \rightarrow \pi ,0$ respectively.
\end{itemize}
\end{cor}

\begin{ex}
(Stress tensor) Let the integrand be denoted 
\[I=-\dfrac{1}{2}\partial _{\mu }\phi \partial ^{\mu }\phi -\alpha R\phi ^2\]
then,
\[\begin{align*}
T_{\mu \nu }=&-\dfrac{2}{\sqrt{-g}}\dfrac{1}{\delta g^{\mu \nu }}\delta (\sqrt{-g})I-\dfrac{2}{\sqrt{-g}}\dfrac{1}{\delta g^{\mu \nu }}\sqrt{-g}\,\delta I\\
=&g_{\mu \nu }I-\dfrac{2}{\delta g^{\mu \nu }}\Big[-\dfrac{1}{2}\partial _{\mu }\phi \partial _{\nu }\phi\,\delta g^{\mu \nu } -\alpha \,\delta g^{\mu \nu }\,R_{\mu \nu }\phi ^2-\alpha g^{\mu \nu }\,\delta R_{\mu \nu }\phi ^2\Big]\\
=&-\dfrac{1}{2}\partial _{\mu }\phi \partial_{\nu }\phi +g_{\mu \nu }\alpha \phi ^2\Big[2R_{\mu \nu }-g_{\mu \nu }R+(g_{\mu \nu }g^{\rho \sigma }\nabla _{\rho }\nabla _{\sigma }-\nabla _{\mu }\nabla _{\nu })\Big]\\
=&-\dfrac{1}{2}\partial _{\mu }\partial _{\nu }\phi +2\alpha \Big[\phi ^2G_{\mu \nu }+(g_{\mu \nu }g^{\rho \sigma }\nabla _{\rho }\nabla _{\sigma }-\nabla _{\mu }\nabla _{\nu })\phi ^2\Big]
\end{align*}\]
In going from lines 2 to 3, we have used the variation of a matrix determinant and the Palatini's identity. Also, from lines 3 to 4, we have used integration by parts as we can assume that the expression is under the integral sign. On-shell, notice that the expression's trace is
\[\mathrm{Tr}(T)=-\dfrac{1}{2}(\nabla \phi )^2+2\alpha \Big[\phi ^2\Big(-\dfrac{D-2}{2}R\Big)+(D-1)g^{\rho \sigma }\nabla _{\rho }\nabla _{\sigma }\phi ^2\Big]\]

\end{ex}
